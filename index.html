5 Golden Rules for Live Coding

Top 1% Standard
  1. 1 Ban iterrows() & apply(axis=1): Always reach for vectorized np.where(), np.select(), or boolean masks first (50x–500x faster).
  2. 2 Master groupby().transform(): Whenever an interviewer asks for "row value divided by group total" (like SQL SUM() OVER (PARTITION BY)), use .transform('sum').

  4. 4 Bitwise Operators & Parentheses: In Pandas masks, always wrap conditions in parentheses with &, |, ~—never Python's and/or.
  5. 5 State Memory Impact: Mention downcasting object columns to category or string[pyarrow] on 10M+ row datasets.
